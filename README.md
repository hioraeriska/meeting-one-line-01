One sentence that replaces the entire meeting: "The client said yes on Tuesday, the budget was approved in the email nobody read, and the only thing left to decide is who cancels the follow-up meeting."

## Why this line works

Every pointless meeting has the same shape: the real decisions were already made somewhere else — a yes on Tuesday, a budget signed off in an email nobody opened — and the meeting itself is just the calendar's way of making everyone watch the decision get re-announced. This line says the quiet part out loud: it delivers both decisions in the first breath, names the unread email everyone recognizes, and leaves the room with exactly one job, which is to cancel the next meeting. After it, there is nothing left to meet about.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
